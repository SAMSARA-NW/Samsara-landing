The Story

Forgotten olive trees.
One purpose.
Bringing back abundance.

Deep in the Swartberg Karoo, 16,000 olive trees were planted years ago with a vision for abundance. Drought, neglect, and hardship left them struggling — less than 1% bearing fruit.

I am bringing them back. Block by block, tree by tree. Every purchase directly funds this restoration.
